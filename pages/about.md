## What this site is

{{name}} is an independent yoga education site. Its goal is simple: explain yoga styles, poses and breathing techniques clearly and safely, so beginners can understand what they are doing and why, without jargon or pressure to be flexible.

## How the guides are written

- Each guide explains one style or technique in detail, including how to do it, common mistakes, modifications and who should take extra care.
- Health information is based on reputable public sources such as the U.S. National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) and MedlinePlus, and links to them appear at the end of each guide.
- I describe what research suggests and where evidence is limited, rather than promising cures.
- Guides are updated when better information becomes available, and corrections are welcome.

## Important note

I am not a medical professional, and nothing here replaces advice from a doctor, physiotherapist or qualified yoga teacher. If you have an injury, a health condition, or you are pregnant, speak to a professional before starting a new practice.

## Advertising and honesty

This site may display advertisements to cover hosting costs. Ads never change what is written, and any sponsored or affiliate content will be clearly labeled.
